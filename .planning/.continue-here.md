<adm_013_decision_log>

These choices were explicitly confirmed with the user and locked during ADM-013 planning:

1) Prior-period comparison base
- Immediate previous equivalent window (not YoY).
- Example: Jan 10-16 compares to Jan 3-9.

2) No date selected fallback
- Rolling last 30 days vs previous 30 days.

3) Card click behavior
- Toggle related filter and preserve all other active filters/search.

4) Revenue rule
- Captured-only for Card Revenue, Cash Revenue, and Total Revenue.

5) Avg tip percent formula
- Average of `tip_amount / amount` for captured card-family payments (ignore zero/NULL amount).

6) Voided/Returned formula
- Combined unique payments where `is_voided OR is_returned`.
- Amount precedence: `return_amount` when returned, else `total_amount` when voided.

7) Total Transactions card click action
- Clear/reset payment method filter to all, preserve all other filters.

8) ADM-015 status decision
- Marked `Done (user-confirmed)`.

</adm_013_decision_log>
