# 02 — Website → Pages

`/brands/{brandId}/website/pages?locationId={loc}` — the hub of the whole feature.

![Website Pages list](../screenshots/01-website-pages-list.png)

---

## 1. Anatomy

```
Pages                                        [ Change Style ] [ New Page ]
Manage pages in your website.

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 🔍 Search                          │ Created by │ Status              │  ← header row
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Home                               │ Owner      │ [Published]         │  ← no chevron
│ Catering                           │ Owner      │ [Published ▾]       │
│ …                                                                     │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
 ‹ 1 2 ›   1-7 of 8
```

- **Title + one-line description.** Every sub-page in this feature follows the same
  `H1 + muted one-liner` pattern (*"Manage pages in your website."*). Cheap, consistent, worth copying.
- **Two header actions**, clearly ranked: `Change Style` secondary/outline, `New Page` primary/blue.
- **Search lives inside the table header**, occupying the first column's header cell rather than sitting
  above the table. Unusual, tidy, and it makes the search visually *belong* to the list it filters.
- **Three columns only**: Name, Created by, Status. No dates, no URL, no author avatars. Restraint.

---

## 2. The `Created by` column

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Owner` | Generated by Owner — either at onboarding, or by their team |
| `—` | Created by the merchant |

Observed: Home, Catering, Our Story, Events, We're Hiring, Gift Cards are all `Owner`; Parties and
Contact Us are `—`.

This is a **done-for-you signal**. Owner builds the initial site for the merchant, so most pages are
theirs; the em-dash marks what the merchant added afterwards. It quietly communicates "we did this for you"
every time the merchant opens the tab.

If we adopt this, the analogous split is *seeded from template* vs *merchant-created*.

---

## 3. Status control

Green `Published` / grey `Unpublished` pill, with a chevron that opens a two-item menu:

![Page status dropdown showing Unpublish and Delete](../screenshots/02-pages-status-dropdown.png)

| Item | Style |
|---|---|
| `⊙ Unpublish` | normal |
| `🗑 Delete` | **red**, destructive |

### Home has no chevron

The `Home` row renders the pill as a plain badge with no menu at all. Home cannot be unpublished and cannot
be deleted.

**This is a real safety rail and we should copy it.** It makes "merchant accidentally takes their whole site
down" structurally impossible rather than merely discouraged. Note it is enforced by *omitting the control*,
not by showing a disabled item or an error toast after the fact.

### Publish state is per page

There is no site-wide publish. Each page carries its own state, and `Events` sits `Unpublished` while the rest
of the site is live — a page can be prepared and parked indefinitely.

---

## 4. Pagination

`‹ 1 2 ›` plus a `1-7 of 8` counter. **7 rows per page** — an aggressively small page size that reveals the
expectation: *a restaurant website is under a dozen pages.* Nobody is building a 200-page site here.

Both the arrows and the numbers are buttons; the current page number is highlighted, and the outer arrows
disable at the ends.

| 2 | **Catering** | Published | 10 | Owner | Marketing page + embedded lead form |
| 3 | **Our Story** | Published | 7 | Owner | Long-form about page |
| 4 | **Events** | Unpublished | 4 | Owner | System page; renders the Events feature |
| 5 | **We're Hiring** | Published | 4 | Owner | System page; renders the Careers feature |
| 6 | **Gift Cards** | Published | 4 | Owner | System page; Buy Card / Check Balance |

Full block-by-block composition: [14 — Page anatomy](14-page-anatomy.md).

### Three classes of page

The list mixes three genuinely different things behind one uniform row, which is elegant:

1. **Content pages** (Home, Catering, Our Story, Parties) — freely composed from sections.
2. **System / feature pages** (Events, We're Hiring, Gift Cards) — a locked hero plus one feature-rendering
   block. Their content comes from Events / Careers / the gift-card system, not from the editor. Almost every
   block on them shows *edit-only or no controls at all*.
3. **Form pages** (Contact Us, Parties) — thin wrappers around a reusable Form object.

**Design consequence:** a "page" in Owner is not always a document. Sometimes it is a *mount point for a feature*.
Our section registry should model that distinction explicitly rather than pretending everything is free-form.

---

## 6. Row interaction

Clicking anywhere in the row (outside the status pill) opens the full-screen page editor at
`/website/pages/{pageId}`. Rows highlight on hover. There is no row-level ⋯ menu — every destructive action
lives behind the status chevron.

---

## 7. What is *not* here

Worth listing, because their absence is the design:

- No page duplication
- No drafts-vs-live column, no "last edited" timestamp, no editor attribution
- No folders, no nesting, no hierarchy
- No per-page SEO fields anywhere in this list (title/description/OG are not exposed)
- No reordering of pages here — page order in the nav is set inside the **Navigation** section editor
  (see [05 — Page editor](05-page-editor.md)), which is a genuinely surprising place to put it
